The Story

Challenge

In a crowded food-tech space, Fantuan needed social content that went beyond promotions — content that genuinely reflected the communities its restaurant partners serve. The goal was to build a consistent visual identity across Instagram, WeChat, and Little Red Book while driving measurable engagement among Chinese-Canadian audiences.

Strategy

Developed platform-specific content systems for each channel — adapting tone, format, and cadence to audience behavior on each platform. Layered in coordinated product photography to elevate restaurant partner content, and activated live brand events to build offline community touchpoints that fed back into digital storytelling.

Outcome

Consistent brand growth across all three platforms, with a 32% average engagement lift driven by high-quality visual content. The Fantuan × Little Red Book event activation created significant organic reach and brand-community association, demonstrating that experiential moments amplify digital performance.
