The Story

Challenge

Limbus Work Collective had no brand, no followers, and no content history — a blank slate entering a competitive Toronto arts scene. The goal was to establish identity, grow a real community, and convert that community into ticket buyers, all within five months.

Strategy

Built the brand architecture first — visual identity, tone, bilingual voice — then launched a phased content rollout: awareness → community → conversion. Deployed simultaneously across Instagram, Facebook, and Little Red Book to reach both English and Chinese-speaking audiences, with weekly A/B testing to compound efficiency.

Outcome

40% higher campaign efficiency over initial benchmarks. Month-over-month CTR and engagement growth through the full run, proving that a zero-budget brand built on strategy and content quality can generate real audience loyalty before a single ticket goes on sale.

My Role

Responsibilities
